New `--argus-radius` and `--argus-outline` tokens. `--argus-radius` rounds the table's corners, and the header, rows and pinned columns are clipped to them. `--argus-outline` draws a border around the whole table, as a `border` shorthand such as `1px solid #ddd`. By default the table stays square with no border.
