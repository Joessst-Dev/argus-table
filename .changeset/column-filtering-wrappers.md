`columnFilters` binds two-way like `columnPinning`: `v-model:column-filters` in Vue, `[(columnFilters)]` in Angular, and `onColumnFilter` in React. `quickFilter` is a plain prop. `<ArgusColumn>` takes `filter`, `filterFn` and `filterOptions`, and the filter types (`ColumnFilter`, `ColumnFilterState`, …) are exported.
