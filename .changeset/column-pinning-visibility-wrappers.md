The `columnPinning` and `columnVisibility` props bind two-way like `columnOrder`: `v-model:column-pinning` and `v-model:column-visibility` in Vue, `[(columnPinning)]` and `[(columnVisibility)]` in Angular, and `onColumnPin` / `onColumnHide` in React. `<ArgusColumn>` takes `pinnable` and `hideable`.
