Column groups: the table takes `columnGroups`, and `ArgusColumnGroup` (`ArgusColumnGroupComponent` in Angular, also in `ARGUS_TABLE`) wraps `ArgusColumn`s as `<argus-column-group>` does. A group's `header` can be framework content, typed as `ReactColumnGroup`, `VueColumnGroup` and `AngularColumnGroup`.
