New Material-inspired theme, `@argus-table/themes/material.css`. It uses the Material 3 baseline colours in light and dark, Roboto, taller rows (40, 52 and 60px by density), an outlined surface with rounded corners and deeper shadows on pinned columns. The theme loads no fonts: load Roboto 400 and 500 yourself, for example from `@fontsource/roboto`. The default theme sets the new `--argus-radius` and `--argus-outline` tokens and looks the same as before.
