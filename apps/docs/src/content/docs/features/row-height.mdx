`wrap: true` on a column (`wrap` on `<argus-column>`) wraps its text onto more lines instead of cutting it off. Rows keep their height: here `rowHeight` 56 leaves room for two lines. Text that fits stays centred; longer text starts at the top and is clipped below.

## Rows that fit their content

`rowHeight: 'auto'` (`row-height="auto"`) fits each row to its content: wrapped text, and content taller than a line, such as the bars here, whose height follows the total. The row height (from `density` or `--argus-row-height`) is then the least a row is, and the header's height. Content sits at the top of its cell, with the padding that centres one line (`--argus-cell-padding-block`). Pinned rows grow too.

Rows are measured as they render, so rows not seen yet count as one line, and the rows in view stay put while rows above them are measured. Give images a size, so rows don't move when they load.
