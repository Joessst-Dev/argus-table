`pinnedRows: { bottom: [totals] }` keeps a totals row at the bottom while the body scrolls. Pinned rows are separate objects, read by the same columns, and never sorted or filtered. Cell content gets `pinned` (`'top'` or `'bottom'`) in its context, so it can show a summary row its own way: in bold here. Pinned rows have the `row-pinned` and `row-pinned-bottom` (or `row-pinned-top`) parts, and their background is `--argus-row-pinned-bg`.

## Both sides, and the keyboard

The keyboard moves through pinned rows like any row: from the header down into the top row, through the body, and on to the bottom row; Ctrl+End lands on the totals. PageUp and PageDown page through the body only. A click, or Enter on a cell, fires `argus-row-click` (and `argus-cell-click`) with `pinned` set to the row's side.

Pinned rows work the same with a [`dataSource`](../infinite-loading/): they don't wait for pages.
