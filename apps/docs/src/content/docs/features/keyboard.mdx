The table follows the WAI-ARIA grid pattern. It is one tab stop, starting on the first header. The arrow keys move between cells, and between the header and the body; Home and End jump to the start or end of the row. PageDown and PageUp move by a page of rows and scroll with it; Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End (Cmd on macOS) jump to the first header and the last cell <Since since="0.2" />. Enter on a body cell fires `argus-cell-click` and `argus-row-click`, as a click does (see the log). Enter or Space sorts a focused sortable header, and Alt+Arrow resizes it. Scroll away and press an arrow key: focus comes back. Directions follow the writing direction, so in a right-to-left table ArrowRight moves toward the start.
