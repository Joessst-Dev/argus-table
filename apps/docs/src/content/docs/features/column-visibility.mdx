`columnPinning` pins and unpins columns over their own `pin`, and `columnVisibility` hides and shows them over their own `hidden` (Status starts hidden here). Both are plain records by column id, so they save as JSON: `false` unpins or hides, and a column not listed keeps its definition. A hidden column still sorts and keeps its place in the order. `hideable: false` keeps a column from being hidden (#), and `pinnable: false` from being pinned; the last shown column can't be hidden either.

Users change both through plugins, such as the column menu of `@argus-table/header-menu`; the table fires `argus-column-pinning-change` and `argus-column-visibility-change` with the proposed records, so the two-way bindings above follow those changes too.

## On column children

With columns as children, the standard `hidden` attribute hides a column, and `pinnable="false"` or `hideable="false"` keep users from pinning or hiding it.
