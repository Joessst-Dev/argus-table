`@argus-table/export` is a plugin: `plugins: [tableExport()]`. `downloadTable(table, 'csv' | 'xlsx' | 'pdf')` saves what the table shows: the columns in their order, the rows sorted and filtered, with the pinned rows and the group headers. `exportTable()` returns the file as a `Blob` instead. Both take the element: through a `ref` in React, a template ref's `element` in Vue, or a view query's `element` in Angular. `rows: 'selected'` exports the selected rows only.

With `headerMenu()` next to it, every column menu offers the formats too. Excel cells keep their types (numbers, dates, booleans), and the header rows and pinned columns are frozen. The Excel and PDF writers load on first use, so the plugin itself stays small.

## Per column

A column's `meta.export` sets how it is exported, next to its definition: `header`, `value` (the value to write, from the one the column reads) and `exclude`. The `columns` option of `tableExport()` or of a call wins field by field. A header that is content names the column through text mode, as in the [header menu](../header-menu/): here Status stays out of the files, Total is exported as whole euros under another header, and Customer keeps its name.

## Detail rows

An export can carry the rows behind each row, an order's lines say, through the `detail` option: their `columns` (`id`, `header`, and optionally `value`, `align`, `width`) and `rows(row, { rowId, signal })`, which returns a row's detail rows or a promise of them. It's called for every exported row, panel open or not, in order and at most six at a time; rows that `rowExpandable` rejects and pinned rows get none. The export doesn't read the [detail panels](../detail-rows/): it writes the data you give it, with or without `rowDetail`.

- **Excel:** each row's detail rows follow it, one column in, under a header of their own, grouped so Excel's outline buttons collapse them. Such a sheet has no filter buttons.
- **PDF:** the same, indented.
- **CSV:** one table: the row's columns, then the detail columns, one line per detail row with the row's values repeated.

With `detail` given to `tableExport()`, the column menu offers the formats twice: Export, without the detail rows, and Export with details. `detail: false` leaves them out of one export.
