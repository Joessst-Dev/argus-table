`width: 'auto'` fits a column to its header, its pinned rows and the rows in view, within `minWidth` and `maxWidth` (Product stops at 360 px here). It measures once the first rows render, then keeps its width, so scrolling never moves the columns. An auto width is the column's own width, not a resize: it fires no event and adds nothing to `columnSizing`.

## Fitting on demand

A double-click on a header's resize handle fits that column to the rows in view, and `autosizeColumns(ids?)` fits several, or all. Both are resizes: they fire `argus-column-sizing-change` for each column they change and follow the `columnSizing` rules, so a saved layout keeps them.
